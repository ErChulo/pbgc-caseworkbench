## PP-01 Eligibility

An employee is eligible to participate on the first day of the month following
completion of one year of service and attainment of age 21. Service is measured
from the date of hire in completed calendar months.

## PP-02 Normal retirement

A participant's normal retirement date is the first day of the month coincident
with or next following the 65th birthday, provided the participant has at least
five years of vesting service. A participant who reaches age 65 with fewer than
five years of vesting service retires on the first day of the month following the
date the fifth year of vesting service is completed.

## PP-03 Accrued benefit formula

The monthly normal retirement benefit is 1.50% of final average monthly
compensation multiplied by years of credited service, with a minimum of $0.

Final average monthly compensation is the average of the highest 60 consecutive
months of compensation during the last 120 months of credited service.

## PP-04 Compensation

Compensation means base wages and salary, excluding overtime, bonuses,
commissions, reimbursements, and employer contributions to any benefit plan.
Compensation is not annualized for a partial year.

## PP-05 Credited service

Credited service is measured in completed years and months from date of hire to
date of termination, retirement, or death, using the plan's anniversary date of
January 1 for partial plan years.

## PP-06 Vesting

Vesting service is credited in the same manner as PP-05, with a maximum of 100
years. The vesting schedule is the 5-15 graded schedule:

| Completed years of vesting service | Vested percentage |
| --- | --- |
| fewer than 5 | 0% |
| 5 | 25% |
| 6 through 10 | 25% plus 5 percentage points per year |
| 11 through 15 | 50% plus 10 percentage points per year |
| 15 or more | 100% |

## PP-07 Early retirement

A participant with at least 10 years of vesting service may retire at age 55.
The benefit is reduced by 0.50% for each complete month between the early
retirement date and age 62. No reduction applies at or after age 62.

## PP-08 Forms of benefit

The normal form for a married participant is a 50% joint and survivor annuity
with the spouse as beneficiary. The normal form for an unmarried participant is
a single life annuity. Optional forms are a single life annuity with 120 monthly
payments certain, and a 75% joint and survivor annuity. Actuarial equivalence is
determined using the plan's current basis, which is not part of this document.

## PP-09 Death benefits

If a vested participant dies before commencing benefits, the surviving spouse
may elect a qualified preretirement survivor annuity. If there is no surviving
spouse, the participant's vested accrued benefit is payable to the designated
beneficiary in a lump sum.

## PP-10 Plan termination

On termination, benefits are determined under the plan as in effect on the date
of plan termination, subject to the applicable requirements of Title IV of ERISA
and the PBGC. This document does not state those requirements and must not be
read as doing so.

## PP-11 Benefit freeze

Effective January 1, 2021, the plan ceased accruing benefits for all participants.
The freeze does not change a participant's accrued benefit as of December 31,
2020, and does not terminate the plan. This synthetic amendment supplies the
Date of Benefit Freeze used by the R5 header.
